## 2026-06-12 - [Multi-layered Guidance & Persona]
**Learning:** Combining emojis, tooltips (`help`), and placeholders creates a multi-layered guidance system that reduces user anxiety when interacting with complex AI systems. Using `st.chat_message` with a consistent persona emoji (🌌) makes the interaction feel conversational rather than transactional.
**Action:** Use emoji prefixes for primary actions (e.g., ✨ Query), provide tooltips for all interactive elements, and wrap AI responses in persona-branded chat blocks.
