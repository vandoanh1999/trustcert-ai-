# Genesis Core V8 - Pitch Deck Outline

This document outlines the 15 slides for the Genesis V8 pitch deck.

---

### Slide 1: Title Slide
*   **Title:** Genesis: The Self-Sustaining AI Network.
*   **Subtitle:** 100x Lighter than Bittensor, with Verifiable Trust Built-In.
*   **Visual:** The V8 Mermaid Architecture Diagram.

### Slide 2: The Problem
*   **Title:** Today's AI is Centralized, Expensive, and Untrustworthy.
*   **Points:**
    *   Massive Compute Walls (e.g., Bittensor) exclude community participation.
    *   Centralized models are a single point of failure and control.
    *   No easy way to verify the quality or reputation of AI agents.

### Slide 3: The Solution
*   **Title:** Genesis: A Lean, Decentralized, and Trustworthy AI Ecosystem.
*   **Points:**
    *   A P2P network of AI experts that anyone can run.
    *   A built-in reputation system (Aurora Trust) that rewards quality.
    *   A network that autonomously discovers knowledge and grows its own intelligence.

### Slide 4: How It Works (The Symbiotic Network)
*   **Visual:** Simplified diagram showing the User Feedback Loop and the Rover Growth Loop.
*   **Explanation:** "Users teach the network what's good, and the network teaches itself what's new."

### Slide 5: Technology: 100x Lighter
*   **Title:** Hyper-Efficient Decentralization.
*   **Points:**
    *   **No Blockchain:** We use a lightweight P2P gossip protocol, not a heavy blockchain.
    *   **Hardware-Based Identity:** Anti-Sybil is achieved through hardware fingerprints, not expensive Proof-of-Work.
    *   **MPC & Shamir's:** Securely manage critical functions without a central authority.

### Slide 6: Technology: Verifiable Trust
*   **Title:** The Aurora Trust Engine.
*   **Points:**
    *   User feedback directly impacts AI expert and node operator reputation scores.
    *   Reputation is portable and verifiable via Verifiable Credentials (VCs).
    *   Super Nodes (the most trusted operators) act as guardians of the network.

### Slide 7: Technology: Autonomous Growth
*   **Title:** The Ecosystem Rover.
*   **Points:**
    *   An AI agent that constantly seeks out new, valuable datasets.
    *   Automatically proposes new AI experts to the network.
    *   Super Nodes vote to approve, and the network auto-trains and deploys the new expert.

### Slide 8: Live Demo Preview
