However, for commercial use, a separate license is required.

## Definitions

*   **"Commercial Use"** is defined as any use of the Software for direct or indirect commercial advantage or monetary compensation. This includes, but is not limited to:
    *   Deploying a private, modified, or fork of the Genesis network for enterprise use.
    *   Selling services that are primarily powered by the Software.
    *   Using the Software as a backend for a commercial application.

## Obtaining a Commercial License

Enterprises and individuals wishing to use the Genesis Core V8 software for Commercial Use must contact the Genesis Core development team to purchase a commercial license.

**Contact:** `licensing@genesis-network.dev` (placeholder)
